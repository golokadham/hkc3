A place to connect, learn, and grow in Krishna consciousness

Hare Krishna Community (HKC) brings people across Bloomington-Normal together through kirtan, sacred texts, prasadam, service, and spiritual friendship.

Whether you are discovering the Bhagavad-gita for the first time or deepening a long-standing devotional practice, you are welcome to join a gathering and take part at your own pace.

Upcoming Major Festivals

March 2027

Gaura Purnima

Appearance day of Lord Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu. Features ecstatic Harinam, Golden Abhishek ceremony, class on divine pastimes, and grand fast-breaking prasadam.

The divine appearance day of Lord Sri Krishna. Non-stop kirtan, midnight Maha-Aarti, cultural drama, 108 bhoga offerings, and midnight feast.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience to attend?

Not at all! Everyone is welcome regardless of background, culture, or prior experience with yoga or meditation.

Is there any fee for attending programs?

All regular programs and prasadam meals are free of charge. Donations are strictly voluntary.

Can I host Bhakti Vriksha at my home?

Yes! Contact us using the form to schedule a home satsang, kirtan, or book distribution event at your home.
